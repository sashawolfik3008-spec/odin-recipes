Crispy Sweet Potato Fries with Cheese Sauce

Home

Description

These oven-baked sweet potato fries are golden on the outside and tender on the inside. Served with a rich, creamy cheddar cheese sauce, they make a delicious and comforting snack.

Perfect for movie nights, gatherings, or a cozy evening at home.

Ingredients

For the Sweet Potato Fries

  • 500 g sweet potatoes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • ½ tsp sweet paprika
  • 2 large eggs
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper (optional)

For the Cheese Sauce

  • 120 g cheddar cheese, grated
  • 150 ml milk
  • 10 g unsalted butter
  • 10 g all-purpose flour
  • A pinch of salt

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C. Line a large baking tray with parchment paper.
  2. Wash and peel the sweet potatoes, then cut them into evenly sized fries.
  3. Pat the fries dry with a clean kitchen towel. Toss them with cornstarch, paprika, and olive oil until evenly coated.
  4. Spread the fries in a single layer on the baking tray, leaving space between them.
  5. Bake for 25-35 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden brown and lightly crispy.
  6. Remove the fries from the oven and sprinkle with salt.
  7. To prepare the cheese sauce, melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium-low heat.
  8. Add the flour and whisk for about 1 minute. Gradually pour in the milk, stirring continuously until the sauce thickens.
  9. Reduce the heat to low. Add the grated cheddar cheese and stir until melted and smooth. Remove from the heat and season with a pinch of salt if needed.
  10. Serve the sweet potato fries immediately with the warm cheese sauce on the side. Enjoy!
